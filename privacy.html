Privacy information

Free preview — information about how the current website works. This page describes observed site functionality; it is not a substitute for a formally reviewed privacy notice.

What stays on your device

Your selected encouragement themes are processed in the browser. Optional reflections, saved thoughts and communication preferences use your browser's local storage. You can clear them through the controls provided on the site or your browser settings. They are not synced to an account.

Notifications and calendar

Calendar reminders are generated as a file on your device. Test notifications require browser permission. Saving a communication preference does not enrol you in email or push messaging.

External resources and hosting

Some landscape images load from Unsplash-hosted image URLs, which means your browser may contact that provider. Cloudflare serves this website and may process technical request information under its service arrangements. Do not enter sensitive personal information into local reflections on a shared device.

Payments and accounts

Paid memberships, checkout, account registration and recurring notifications are not active. KINDELORA does not request payment card details through this website.

Questions and corrections

A verified support contact and formal data-controller details are being prepared before wider public launch. Please do not submit personal or sensitive information until a suitable contact channel is published.
