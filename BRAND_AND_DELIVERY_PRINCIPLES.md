# KINDELORA — Brand & Delivery Principles

## Golden thread for every business
- GitHub-first version control and Cloudflare Pages deployment, with safe rollback.
- Public-facing experiences, mobile-first accessibility, clear guest navigation, fast performance.
- Zero-cost infrastructure unless founder explicitly approves expenditure.
- Each business has isolated source, credentials, apps, data, deployment and branding.
- WOW + HEART + TRUST: premium, human, warm, respectful and non-manipulative.
- Protect privacy and comply with applicable laws before collecting customer information.
- CI checks, link and functional testing, mobile and PWA QA before declaring a release complete.
- Never change KINDRA, VELORA or Oppel Enterprises while working on KINDELORA.

## Distinctive brand positioning
- Oppel Enterprises: professional navy/gold.
- VELORA: cinematic violet/rose/gold.
- KINDRA: forest green/ivory/coral, AI companionship.
- KINDELORA: kindness and uplift; proposed midnight teal #123B45, sea-glass #C9E7DE, sunrise amber #E9A34A, cream #FFF9EE.
- Proposed colours are not yet approved for production; avoid visual confusion with other businesses.

## Guest and membership promises
- Useful free experience with no mandatory sign-up.
- Show empathy without forced positivity or pressure to disclose feelings.
- Proposed Plus $4.99/month or $49.99/year upfront, a $9.89 (16.5%) saving compared with twelve monthly payments.
- No active checkout, payment capture or subscriber data until security, policy and compliance gates are complete.

## Cloudflare migration guardrails
- Existing kindelora.pages.dev is a Direct Upload project; do not delete it or assume Git integration can be added in-place.
- Create and verify a separate Git-integrated Pages project before cutover, preserving the current production website and rollback route.
