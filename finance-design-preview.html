Automation & AI blueprint

Deterministic automation

Verified payment → atomic ledger entry → invoice PDF → email outbox → customer download → settlement matching. Retries and alerts are idempotent.

AI-assisted exceptions

Future AI may summarise unreconciled items and suggest matches using redacted data. It must never approve refunds, change bank details, grant access or post accounting adjustments without explicit authorised controls.
